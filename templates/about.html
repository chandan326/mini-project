About AgriHealth AI

Designing accessible, reliable, and scalable agricultural technology for farmers.

Our Core Mission

Agricultural disease outbreaks cause significant crop yield loss annually. AgriHealth AI bridges the gap between field observation and verified agricultural science by providing a simple, mobile-friendly platform where farmers can upload plant photos, report symptoms, and receive AI-assisted disease assessments with practical management guidance.

Key Design Principles

  • Farmer-First Usability: Large touch targets, minimal typing, and clear visuals.
  • Ensemble Reliability: Multi-image analysis avoids false positives from single obscured leaf photos.
  • Verified Knowledge Base: Explanations and care instructions sourced from verified agricultural reference material.
  • Safety & Transparency: Low confidence flags ensure uncertain predictions are never presented as guaranteed.
