Meet AgriHealth AI. Explore changes in your plants with photo-based guidance for farmers and gardeners.

Search for your crop in English or Hindi. Choose from thirty major Indian crops. Select one to move straight to photos.

Add one to five clear photos from your gallery, or use your camera. Show leaves, stems, and affected areas in good light.

Answer a few questions about symptoms, weather, and when changes began. Gemini reviews your photos and answers together for an AI assessment.

Read the possible condition, visible signs, and safe crop-care steps. Gemini can suggest conditions beyond the local disease guides. Download your PDF.

AI guidance is not a confirmed diagnosis. Consult a local agricultural expert before treatment decisions. Start with clear photos, and keep monitoring your plants.
