How AgriHealth AI Works

Understanding our 5-image ensemble pipeline & verified agricultural knowledge base matching.

1

5-Photo Multi-Angle Upload

Single leaf photos can often obscure early disease progression. Farmers upload up to 5 photos capturing affected leaf surfaces, stem junctions, or whole plant structures.

2

Image Preprocessing & Blur Detection

Each uploaded image is validated for resolution, contrast, and sharpness. Blurry or overexposed images trigger warning notices to encourage clearer capture.

3

ML Ensemble Prediction

Visual pattern features from valid photos are evaluated against disease classification models. Probabilities across images are aggregated for consistency.

4

Questionnaire Correlation

Farmer inputs regarding weather (humidity, rain), symptom onset, and spreading status are correlated with visual ML predictions to compute final confidence.
